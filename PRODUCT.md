# Clippify

## Register

product

## Users

Creators turning interviews, podcasts, gameplay, and other footage into short-form clips, and editors working in the full desktop timeline.

## Purpose

On a phone, select one video and get one shareable vertical cut. Keep advanced editing in the desktop workspace.

## Personality

Cinematic, capable, and straightforward. Use a familiar editor's visual language with the simple flow the mobile brief calls for.

## Anti-references

Do not shrink a desktop timeline into the default phone experience. Avoid multi-step forms, simulated results, and visual clutter that competes with the video.

## Design principles

- One primary action per phase: select, create, then save.
- Lead with the actual video. Keep optional settings out of the way.
- State when media is uploaded and distinguish upload, processing, and export.

## Accessibility

Provide Arabic RTL, readable contrast, scalable type, clear focus, labeled controls, and a reduced-motion path.
